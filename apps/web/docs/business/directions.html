JOEYY for business: three directions

The site an owner uses to sign up, make their card, set up their stands and see how it's going. All three keep the passport navy, the serif and the Oswald figures, so it feels like the other side of the same product. They differ in what comes first.

A. The card desk

Laptop first. The card sits big on the right and redraws as you type; every setting sits in one column on the left. Good for an owner fiddling with colours at home. Weak on a phone, where the preview drops below the form.

B. One question at a time

Phone first, because the kit's QR code opens on the owner's phone, stand in hand. Each screen asks one thing in big serif ("What do people get?"), and the card underneath fills in as they answer. Progress is a row of stamp slots that fill as steps finish. The last step is tapping the stand itself.
